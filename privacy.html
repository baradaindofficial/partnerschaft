2. What We Collect

Depending on your interaction with our platform, we may collect:

  • Identity & Contact: Name, designation, mobile number, email address

We process your data exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Communicating platform updates, registration confirmations, and query responses

We rely on your explicit consent (provided at registration) as the lawful basis for processing. You may withdraw consent at any time — see Section 6.

4. Disclosure of Your Data

Your vendor profile — including contact details, service categories, credentials, and uploaded documents — will be shared with verified brands and companies registered on this platform for the limited purpose of vendor discovery and shortlisting.

We do not:

  • Sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties

We may disclose data to competent authorities if required by law, court order, or government directive.

5. Retention Period

Your data is retained for a period of up to 3 years from the date of registration, or until you withdraw consent — whichever is earlier. After this period, your data will be securely deleted or anonymised.

OTP records are retained for 24 hours and then purged automatically.

  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your data (subject to legal obligations)
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw your consent at any time; this will not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: Lodge a complaint with our Grievance Officer or the Data Protection Board of India
